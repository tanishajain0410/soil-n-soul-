# Image Guidelines for the Website

This document explains where to save your actual images, what to name them, and the recommended dimensions for the best appearance on the website.

---

## 📸 Hero Slider Images (Home Page)

**Path:** `client/public/images/hero/`

The hero slider on the homepage expects exactly **3 images**.
Replace the placeholder files with your actual images using these exact names:

1. **`hero-1.jpg`**
2. **`hero-2.jpg`**
3. **`hero-3.jpg`**

**Recommended Size:**
- **Dimensions:** 1920px width × 1080px height (16:9)
- **File Size:** Under 300KB each
- **Content:** Visually stunning images representing the spiritual, cultural, and historic essence of Varanasi.

---

## 📸 About Us Section Image

**Path:** `client/public/images/`

The "About Us" section on the homepage uses one tall image displayed on the right side of the story text.

**Filename:** **`lordshiv.jpg`**

**Recommended Size:**
- **Dimensions:** 900px width × 1200px height (Portrait/Tall ratio)
- **File Size:** Under 250KB
- **Content:** A spiritual, evocative image related to Varanasi / Lord Shiva / Kashi that represents the soul of the brand.

---

## 📸 Services Images (Home Page & Service Detail Pages)

**Path:** `client/public/images/services/`

Each service has one image. Replace the placeholder files with your actual photos using these exact names:

1. **`service-travel.jpg`** — Travel (Car, Bike, Boat)
2. **`service-stay.jpg`** — Stay
3. **`service-pooja-booking.jpg`** — Pooja Booking
4. **`service-event.jpg`** — Event
5. **`service-city-tour.jpg`** — City Tour

**Recommended Size:**
- **Dimensions:** 800px width × 600px height (4:3 Aspect Ratio)
- **File Size:** Under 150KB
- **Content:** Vibrant, professional photos representing the service (e.g., a boat on the Ganges for Travel, a puja flame for Pooja Booking, a ghat walk for City Tour).

---

## Instructions for replacing:

1. Prepare your images at the recommended dimensions above.
2. Save them as `.jpg` files.
3. Name them **exactly** as listed above (e.g., `service-travel.jpg`).
4. Overwrite (replace) the existing empty placeholder files in the respective folders.
5. The website will automatically display your new images!
